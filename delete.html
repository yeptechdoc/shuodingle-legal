删除账号 · Delete Account

开发者：YepTech · 应用：说定了 Shuodingle（com.mysapps.shuodingle）

一、说定了的"账号"是什么

说定了不需要注册，不用手机号或邮箱。你第一次起昵称时，App 会自动向我们的服务器申请一个随机的匿名设备身份——这就是你的账号，跟你的真实身份没有任何关联。你的昵称、加入/创建的约定、点亮记录都保存在我们的服务器上，这样约定里的朋友之间才能互相看到进度。

二、在 App 内删除账号（推荐，立即生效）

打开说定了，进入「我的 → 关于 → 删除账号」，二次确认后会立即：

  • 让你退出所有加入或创建的约定（一个约定如果因此空了，会连同它的记录一起删除）；
  • 永久删除你的昵称，以及这个匿名设备身份在我们服务器上的所有数据。

这个操作不可恢复。

三、只是卸载 App 呢？

单纯卸载不会删除服务器上的数据——因为你在的约定可能还有其他人在用。如果你想彻底删除，请在卸载前先在 App 内完成上面的删除账号操作。

四、无法进入 App？邮件申请删除

如果已经卸载或进不去 App，可以发邮件到 461812195@qq.com，主题写「删除账号」，并说明你还记得的约定名称/邀请码方便我们核实。我们会在 7 个工作日内处理并回复确认。

Delete Account

Developer: YepTech · App: Shuodingle (com.mysapps.shuodingle)

1. What "account" means in Shuodingle

Shuodingle needs no sign-up, no phone number or email. The first time you set a nickname, the app automatically requests a random, anonymous device identity from our server — that's your account, with no link to your real identity. Your nickname, the pacts you've joined or created, and your check-in history are stored on our server so friends in the same pact can see each other's progress.

2. Delete your account in the app (recommended, takes effect immediately)

Open Shuodingle, go to "You → About → Delete account", confirm, and this immediately:

  • leaves every pact you created or joined (deleting a pact outright, records and all, if that leaves it empty);
  • permanently deletes your nickname and everything tied to this anonymous device identity on our server.

This cannot be undone.

3. What about just uninstalling?

Uninstalling alone does not delete server-side data — other people may still be relying on the pacts you're in. Delete your account in the app first if you want it fully gone.

4. Can't get into the app? Request deletion by email

If you've already uninstalled or can't sign in, email 461812195@qq.com with subject "Delete Account", mentioning any pact name/invite code you remember so we can verify it's you. We reply within 7 business days.
